Computational graph unfolding, sequence dependency modeling, and LSTM gating mechanisms.

A & B. Unfolding Computational Graphs

An unrolled RNN computes representations across discrete time steps \(t \in \{1, \dots, T\}\). Information flows forward through the hidden state vector \(h_t\), parametrized by recurrent weight matrices:

h_t = tanh(W_xh · x_t + W_hh · h_{t-1} + b_h)
y_t = softmax(W_hy · h_t + b_y)

Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT): Errors at step \(T\) propagate backward across previous timesteps. Repeated multiplications by \(W_{hh}\) cause the gradient to either decay exponentially (vanishing gradient) or explode (exploding gradient). LSTMs solve vanishing gradients via constant error carousels (cell states).

Unrolled Graph Architecture
x₁ → [h₁] → y₁
↓
x₂ → [h₂] → y₂
↓
x₃ → [h₃] → y₃
Recurrent Weight Matrix \(W_{hh}\) Shared Across All Time Steps

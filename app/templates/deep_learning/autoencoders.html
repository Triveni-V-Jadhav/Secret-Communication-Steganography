D. Denoising Autoencoder (DAE)

A DAE corrupts training inputs with Gaussian noise \(\tilde{x} \sim \mathcal{N}(x, \sigma^2)\) and trains to project the corrupted sample back onto the clean data manifold, learning invariant semantic features.
